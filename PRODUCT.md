# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Vite with React and TypeScript, chosen for a lightweight static build, a familiar component model, and a straightforward path to add React Router only when the site gains another route.

## Users

Potential collaborators, peers, and people from Austin’s professional or fire-service communities who want a concise introduction to his work.

## Product Purpose

Give Austin a current personal home on `austinkostreba.dev`: a playful, minimal business card that introduces both sides of his working life without publishing personal contact details. Success means a visitor understands either role within a few seconds.

## Positioning

Austin is a software engineering manager at Renaissance, working on one part of an MTSS application. He is one of several engineering managers, not the leader of engineering across Renaissance MTSS products. His story connects hands-on software work with the human and organizational work of building systems that support educators and students.

On nights and weekends, Austin is a paid-on-call firefighter with West Metro Fire-Rescue District, which serves New Hope and Crystal, Minnesota.

## Operating Context

The site is a simple public identity page. It should be useful when shared directly, opened from a profile, or discovered through the domain. It should not imply that Austin is currently seeking work or offering consulting services.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One static, responsive page at launch.
- No blog, project archive, headshot, contact form, or public email yet.
- LinkedIn is the only external destination and appears in Day mode.
- The structure should allow future writing or project pages without requiring a redesign.
- Deployment should work with Cloudflare Pages and the existing Cloudflare-managed domain.
- The current scope contains no analytics, cookies, backend, or third-party embeds.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Austin Kostreba.
- Voice: understated, direct, warm, and credible.
- The visual experience must be extremely subtle and elegant.
- Evan You’s concise personal homepage is a binding reference for restraint and density; Lee Robinson’s site is a reference for adding one memorable visual gesture without overwhelming the biography.
- No headshot.
- Stone Rings is the chosen visual world: warm off-white paper, five tactile plum-and-terracotta ring forms, and a restrained serif-led type pairing.
- On desktop, five artwork forms drift independently by a few pixels and respond subtly to nearby mouse movement. Day uses the original stone rings; Night uses purpose-built charcoal and ember forms over a soft environmental coal bed. Mobile omits decorative artwork to preserve the compact business-card experience. No water or fluid effects. Reduced-motion visitors receive still artwork.
- A Day/Night switch changes between Austin’s Renaissance and firefighter introductions. Night uses a dark paper treatment and an authored transition while preserving the same physical-card identity.

## Product Principles

1. Say only what is known and make every sentence earn its place.
2. Put Austin’s current work and professional direction ahead of a chronological résumé.
3. Use craft and interaction to add personality without adding content noise.
4. Keep the first release fast, accessible, and easy to extend.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, full keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, and a motion-reduced experience for visitors who request it.
